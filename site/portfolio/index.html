How I work

How I usually add value.

I clarify the real workflow behind a request: who owns the data, where handoffs happen, what exceptions matter, and what users need to see when they make decisions.

Then I turn that into useful delivery artifacts: user stories, acceptance criteria, process maps, CRM behavior, automation rules, test scenarios, and rollout notes.
