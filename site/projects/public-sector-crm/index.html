Public sector · Dynamics 365 cloud migration

Modernizing a provincial public-sector CRM for a cloud-first operating model

A transformation program shaped around service continuity, platform resilience, and a roadmap for future capabilities — not only a technical migration.

The program supported more than 500 users and carried years of institutional knowledge inside the CRM. The business challenge was to modernize the foundation without forcing the organization to relearn how to serve people.

My role connected the service model with delivery: clarifying the business gaps, coordinating the modernization roadmap, preparing users for change, and helping leadership make release and adoption decisions with confidence.

Business context

A provincial team needed to move a highly customized CRM from on-premises operations into a more resilient cloud foundation without disrupting frontline service.

Scale

500+ users · long-lived case and service data

Role perspective

Business analysis and functional delivery across discovery, process gaps, release readiness, UAT, and stakeholder coordination.

Outcome

Smoother go-live, faster page loads, and a more stable platform for future Dynamics 365 and Power Platform upgrades.
