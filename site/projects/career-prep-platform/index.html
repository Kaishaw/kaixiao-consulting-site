Career Prep Platform

A product-style experiment in turning unstructured information into a bounded, useful AI workflow.

Project story

The demo started with a simple question: how can an AI workflow turn scattered career information into preparation that feels structured, useful, and still owned by the person using it?

The setting

A job seeker may have a job description, resume, target company, role context, and personal notes spread across different places. The challenge was not generating more text; it was creating a guided path from raw material to something a person could actually practice.

Why now

I wanted a public, self-serve project that showed how an AI capability could be shaped into a bounded product experience rather than presented as an open-ended chatbot.

The tension

The workflow had to be useful without pretending to know more than the input supported. It also had to be simple enough to try, controlled enough to operate publicly, and clear enough that a user could see where AI helped and where human judgment remained necessary.

People in the system

Every project has more than one point of view.

The person preparing

A clear path from a job description and personal experience to focused practice, not a large block of generic advice.

The product experience

A sequence of steps, prompts, and outputs that makes the AI's role understandable and keeps the user moving.

The system owner

Reasonable usage boundaries, reliable deployment, and enough structure to improve the workflow based on real use.

The journey

How the work moved from problem to practice.

  1. 01

    Start with the user's material

    The workflow accepts the job description, target role, company context, and resume information that a person already has, then organizes it around the preparation goal.

  2. 02

    Make the transformation visible

    Instead of hiding the prompt work behind one button, the experience gives the user a sequence: understand the target, identify likely themes, generate practice, and review the result.

  3. 03

    Bound the useful answer

    The output is shaped around interview preparation rather than unlimited content generation, which makes it easier to review, refine, and compare with the source material.

  4. 04

    Ship, observe, and iterate

    Public deployment, usage limits, user flow, and future provider integration are treated as part of the project—not as details that begin after the demo is finished.

Decisions that shaped it

The turning points were business decisions as much as system decisions.

Build a workflow, not a chatbot

The product value comes from the sequence of tasks and decisions around the model, not only from the model's ability to produce fluent text.

Keep the user in the loop

The user remains responsible for choosing what is accurate, relevant, and worth practicing. AI helps organize and expand the material; it does not silently become the user's history.

Treat operations as product design

Deployment, usage limits, provider choices, and a clear public entry point determine whether a useful idea can actually be tried by another person.

Ways to read the work

The same project can reveal different kinds of value.

Product lens

Reduce the distance between information and action

The experience is designed around the user's next useful step: understand, practice, review, and improve.

Delivery lens

Make the idea easy to try

A usable demo needs a clear entry point, bounded interaction, visible output, and an iteration path—not only a technically interesting model call.

Systems lens

Keep AI inside a defined boundary

Inputs, prompts, outputs, limits, deployment, and future provider integration form a small but understandable operating model for the experiment.

What changed

Outcomes are easier to trust when the evidence is visible.

User journey

Structured input becomes a multi-step interview-preparation experience rather than an unbounded prompt box.

Product evidence

The project makes prompt framing, user flow, output boundaries, deployment, and iteration visible enough to discuss.

Business outcome

A public demonstration of how applied AI can turn unstructured information into a focused, useful workflow.

Reflection

A convincing AI demo is not the one that says the most. It is the one that helps someone take a better next step and understand why the result is useful.
