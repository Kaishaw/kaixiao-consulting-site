Copilot in Dynamics 365 is only useful when the business process is clear

Copilot and AI agents can accelerate work inside Dynamics 365, but only when ownership, data, and operating rules are clear enough to trust.

Copilot and agent-style features are becoming part of Dynamics 365 sales and service work, but AI cannot fix unclear ownership, duplicated customer data, or messy escalation rules.

My view: before asking Copilot to summarize, route, recommend, or act, teams need a clean operating model and enough trust in the Dynamics 365 data.
