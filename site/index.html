About me

A systems person who still starts with people.

I started from business and systems analysis, and over time moved deeper into CRM, customer service, sales process design, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform. What I enjoy most is helping people describe what they need clearly enough that a solution can be designed, built, tested, and adopted.

My background includes CRM modernization, public-sector cloud migration, enterprise-wide Dynamics 365 rollouts, lead scoring automation, and BI-enabled operations. I am now especially interested in how Copilot, agents, and applied AI can become useful inside real business processes rather than remain isolated technology experiments.

Now

Exploring what useful AI looks like in real operations.

Right now I am interested in how Copilot and AI agents can work with Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and clearer business processes — without making systems harder to trust.
