Industry notes

Industry notes on CRM, Power Platform, and AI.

A short, practical view of what is changing in customer operations and what still needs good process design.

01

AI in CRM is only useful when the business process is clear

Before asking AI to summarize, route, or recommend, teams need a clean operating model and enough trust in the CRM data.

02

Contact centers are moving closer to CRM and knowledge management

Service work is no longer only about answering cases. It is about connecting case history, customer profile, knowledge, channels, and follow-up actions in one flow.

03

Power Platform is becoming the business team's extension layer

Low-code tools are useful when they sit close to a real business pain, but governance and simple design standards still matter.

04

The best functional consultants translate between people

A lot of value comes from making translation visible and testable across business, delivery, testing, and leadership.
